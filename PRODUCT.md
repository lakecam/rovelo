# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Designers, product managers and engineers who plan work as flows: user flows, task flows and process diagrams. They want diagrams that look on-brand when shared, and a to-do list that lives on the diagram instead of in a separate tool.

## Product Purpose

Rovelo is a local-first desktop app, written entirely in Rust, for drawing branded diagrams and tracking the to-dos attached to each step. Success means a diagram looks like it came from the team's own brand kit in one click, canvas interaction feels immediate, text and canvas round-trip, and the checklist is always one glance away from the step it belongs to.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm and confident. A trusted instrument for people who care how things look: approachable without being playful, capable without visual noise.

## Anti-references

Decorative glass on chrome (glass belongs to diagram groups only), ornamental gradients, oversized rounding, modal-heavy workflows, heavy toolbars that eat the canvas, default-grey GUI toolkit chrome.

## Design Principles

1. Canvas first: the diagram owns the window; chrome floats over it and gets out of the way.
2. One model, many views: canvas, text, and the to-do list are three views of one document.
3. The diagram is the brand sample: the app's own chrome stays neutral so the user's brand kit is the only strong colour on screen.
4. Progressive power: common actions are obvious; advanced controls appear in context, beside the selection.
5. Honest and recoverable: every gesture is one undo step; nothing destructive without undo.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for chrome. Every action has a keyboard path and a visible focus ring on the single focused control; state never relies on colour alone; text contrast at least 4.5:1.

_Assumptions: written from the user's brief and the open-source project's own product notes; no interview was held._
